poe-layouts (static build) - fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games.
It reads your Client.txt locally in the browser; nothing is uploaded.

Data sources (see docs/data-sources.md in the poe-layouts repository):
- Layout images and leveling route: Lailloken/Exile-UI
- Areas, connections, acts: HeartofPhos/exile-leveling
- Zone hints and notes: JusKillmeQik/PoE-Leveling-Guide
